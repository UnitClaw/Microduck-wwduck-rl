# Duck hardware shopping list and total

[Back to the full parts and tools guide](README.en.md#shopping-list)

**One duck with 15 HD1910 servos, the ZERO 3W, NP-F550 batteries, camera, ToF module and speaker, plus our HAT and IMU boards.** HAT and IMU each appear as one complete-board item here; their original detailed BOMs remain in the full guide.

**This is a historical-price purchasing estimate including spare stock.** Bearings, wires, screws and connectors use the reference purchase quantities shown below. Printing equipment, 3D-printed parts, printing materials, build tools, and soldering/cleaning consumables are excluded. New-order shipping is separate; the allocated HAT/IMU costs retain their historical shipping share. Chinese variant labels are retained for matching seller pages.
